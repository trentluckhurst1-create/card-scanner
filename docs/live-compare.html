How the price comparison reads

Each group keeps the Cheapest listing visible beside the Next best alternative. Group summaries also retain Median, Highest and Price spread context so you can see whether the cheapest active ask is genuinely unusual.

Why you may see zero exact-card matches

That does not mean the scanner has no cards. It means the current stores do not simultaneously hold the exact same canonical card identity. Related products remain visible, but are explicitly labelled as variants or player/year context instead of being misrepresented as exact equivalents.

Governance: every price shown here is an active asking price only. It is not fair value. No BUY or STRONG BUY can be created from this page without genuine governed sold evidence.
