SportsCardsPro market evidence

Card Scanner uses SportsCardsPro as an external research destination, not as an acquisition retailer.

Exact-card rule: 1/10 and 8/10 are the same numbered card variant for comparison; /10 and /25 are different variants.

Why the prices are not copied into Card Scanner

SportsCardsPro owns its price data. Its terms allow external references with attribution and a visible link, but prohibit third-party software from redistributing the pricing data without express written permission. Card Scanner therefore links you to SportsCardsPro for the relevant exact-card research instead of scraping or republishing its prices.

Future authorized integration

SportsCardsPro documents a paid API and daily CSV price download. If authorized API access and public-display permission are obtained, Card Scanner can integrate current condition values directly. Their documented API/CSV does not provide historic sales, so recent-sale automation would require a separately authorized method.
